What to know about a temporary flight reservation

Requirements vary by destination, visa category, consulate, and individual circumstances. Confirm the exact requirements with the relevant official authority before you apply.

01

Check the requirement

Review the official embassy, consulate, or immigration checklist to see whether an itinerary, reservation, or fully ticketed booking is required.

02

Verify the details

Make sure traveler names, dates, route, and booking status are accurate and consistent with the rest of your application.

03

Understand validity

Reservations can expire or change. Check validity and confirm whether the receiving authority accepts the document you plan to submit.

Important travel-document note

A reservation or itinerary is not the same as a paid, ticketed flight. Do not describe a reservation as a confirmed ticket unless a ticket has actually been issued. No reservation guarantees visa approval or entry; decisions rest with the relevant authorities.

Frequently asked questions

General information only; always follow the current instructions for your application.

Is a flight reservation the same as a flight ticket?

No. A reservation or itinerary may show planned travel details, while a ticketed booking indicates that a ticket has been issued. Confirm what your application specifically requires.

Will a reservation guarantee visa approval?

No. Visa decisions depend on the full application and the applicable rules. A flight document alone cannot guarantee approval.

Where should I check the requirements?

Use the official website of the embassy, consulate, immigration authority, or visa application center responsible for your application.
